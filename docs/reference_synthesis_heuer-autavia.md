## Model context

The vintage Heuer Autavia is a motorsport-oriented chronograph line whose name combines AUTomobile and AVIAtion. It began in 1933 as a dashboard timer for racing and aviation, and in 1962 became the first Heuer wrist chronograph to carry a model name. The model line covers manual-wind screw-back chronographs (ref. 2446, 3646), snap-back 'compressor' cases (2446C, 7763C, 7863C), and the larger automatic cases (1163, 11630, 11063) that housed the Calibre 11 and its derivatives — Heuer's contribution to the 1969 automatic-chronograph race. The Autavia's defining feature across every execution is the rotating bezel (minutes, hours, MH, tachymeter, decimal-minutes, or 24-hour GMT), used as a functional timing tool alongside the chronograph. The watch was widely worn by professional racers (Jochen Rindt, Mario Andretti, Jo Siffert, Derek Bell, Gilles Villeneuve, Clay Regazzoni), which together with strong U.S. distribution and the 1972 Viceroy cigarette promotion made the Autavia one of Heuer's flagship products through the 1960s and 1970s. The focus references — 2446 (and its variants/executions) and 1163 (Calibre 11/12 automatic) — anchor the two great eras of the line.

## Agreements & conflicts (4)

- **Whether the round-typeface 2446M sub-register dials were deliberate or accidental** — Catalog and 1966 advertising artwork show round-numeral dials, and serials cluster tightly (~85k–89k), so the round-typeface variant is genuine but its rationale remains unresolved.  ·  _scope: 2446_
  - Jeff Stein suggests possible explanations including a Heuer/Singer design trial or printing error
  - Typographer Björn Altmann argues batch production using a still-available pad-printing device of older Helvetica-derived numerals (~30 per batch) is plausible
  - Both agree the dials are factory-correct rather than later assemblies
- **Production volume of round-typeface 2446Ms** — No production records survive; both figures are extrapolations.  ·  _scope: 2446_
  - Altmann estimates roughly 30 per batch
  - Stein estimates approximately 200–300 total from observed serial-number range and Heuer survival rates
- **Movement of an early 'Pre-Viceroy' — original Cal. 11 or service-swapped Cal. 12** — Service swaps to the upgraded Cal. 12 are widely reported as common period practice on these watches.  ·  _scope: 1163_
  - The dealer believes the Cal. 12 in one 'Pre-Viceroy' was a service replacement for an original Cal. 11
  - Other Pre-Viceroy/early 1163 examples sold by the same retailer are described as having the original Cal. 11-I
- **Whether all 1163 bezels were tachymeter as originally delivered** — Bezel originality on individual 1163s often cannot be proven absolutely.  ·  _scope: 1163_
  - Sales descriptions note that the 1163 'was typically fitted with a tachymeter bezel'
  - Period buyers reportedly requested bezel changes (e.g. MH bezels) at point of sale or service

## Stories worth telling (10)

- **Jo Siffert and the white-dial 1163** — Heuer's 1969 sponsorship of Swiss F1 driver Jo Siffert put the white/blue 1163 on the wrists of TV viewers — the 'Siffert' nickname stuck and remains the most recognised automatic Autavia colour scheme.  
  [source](https://shop.hodinkee.com/collections/vintage-watches/products/2700-heuer-ref-1163-siffert)  ·  _scope: 1163_
- **The 1972 Viceroy cigarette promotion** — A $88 promotional Autavia (vs. $200 retail) traded via Parnelli Jones magazine ads and a 10-pack carton tab put thousands of 1163s into U.S. hands during the Quartz Crisis — the largest single-model run of any vintage Heuer chronograph, and the reason the 'Viceroy' is the most accessible 1163 today.  
  [source](https://www.onthedash.com/serial-numbers-of-viceroy-autavias/)  ·  _scope: 1163_
- **Jochen Rindt and the 2446 Mark 3** — The 1970 F1 World Champion (posthumous) wore the 2nd-execution 2446 with the 60-minute bezel and Gay Frères beads-of-rice bracelet; the watch carries his name and won the 2016 Autavia Cup public vote that determined the 2017 reissue.  
  [source](https://www.fratellowatches.com/tbt-heuer-autavia-2446-jochen-rindt/)  ·  _scope: 2446_
- **Mario Andretti and the two-register Autavia** — Andretti — winner of both the Indy 500 and the F1 World Championship — was repeatedly photographed wearing a two-register tachymeter-bezel 2446, anchoring the 'Andretti' nickname.  
  [source](https://www.onthedash.com/heuer-100-guide/)  ·  _scope: 2446_
- **Why the red GMT sub-register hands disappeared** — Pilot Heinz Rengel told Heuer that under red cockpit night-lighting the red chronograph hands on his 1163 GMT vanished against the dial; Heuer changed back to black sub-register hands and added red-edged main hands for night legibility.  
  [source](https://www.onthedash.com/autavia-gmt-red-needles/)  ·  _scope: 1163_
- **Finding the original Chronomatic Autavia from the 1969 launch ads** — In ~2014 a Swiss collector bought a small box of Heuer parts in La Chaux-de-Fonds and reassembled the very Chronomatic Autavia shown in Heuer's March 1969 press release announcing the automatic chronograph — confirmed by dial 'fingerprints' (Chronomatic typeface, shorter 2/4/8/10 markers, distinctive seconds-hand tail).  
  [source](https://www.onthedash.com/found-first-chronomatic-autavia/)  ·  _scope: 1163_
- **Jerry Seinfeld and the Siffert on bracelet** — Jerry Seinfeld wears a Siffert 1163 on its Gay Frères beads-of-rice bracelet across episodes of 'Comedians in Cars Getting Coffee' — a quiet, recurring TV cameo for the model.  
  [source](https://shop.hodinkee.com/collections/vintage-watches/products/2700-heuer-ref-1163-siffert)  ·  _scope: 1163_
- **The Pawn Stars 'Siffert'** — In 2014 a one-owner early 'Siffert' 1163 with original box, papers, and rare decimal-minutes bezel was sold on the U.S. TV show Pawn Stars for $8,000; OnTheDash estimated real market value at $12,000–$14,000 — a useful public datapoint for the model's value and condition factors.  
  [source](https://www.onthedash.com/pawn-stars-autavia/)  ·  _scope: 1163_
- **The Kenyan Air Force 'Shauntavias'** — Three Autavia 73663s issued by the Kenyan Air Force, with radially-arranged Arabic numerals, surfaced via UK collector Shaun Wainstein — a reminder that the Autavia had genuine military issue outside the better-known IDF examples.  
  [source](https://www.onthedash.com/autavia-50th-birthday-scrapbook/)  ·  _scope: model-wide_
- **The Heuer–Abercrombie & Fitch sporting partnership** — From the 1940s, Heuer supplied chronographs to A&F's Madison Avenue shop — including dashboard Autavia timers — for clients like Hemingway, Earhart and several U.S. Presidents; the same partnership produced the Seafarer line, which later shared cases with Autavia references including the 2446.  
  [source](https://shop.hodinkee.com/collections/vintage-watches/products/1535-heuer-autavia-a-f-timer-with-box)  ·  _scope: model-wide_

## Module candidates — future nodes (8)
